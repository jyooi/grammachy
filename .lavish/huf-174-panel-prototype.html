Decide the panel shape

Flip the variants with the bottom bar or the arrow keys. Each variant renders the same Check result. Each uses the Issue contract from HUF-173 and computes the Corrected text the same way. Pick one, or name the pieces to combine. The answer goes into the ticket resolution.

Long Selection

Two knobs cover this. "Long text" is under the limit: the panel shows every Issue. "Too much text" is over the limit: the CLI answers text_too_long and the panel shows an error card with a size bar and an offer to check only the first N characters. That offer re-runs the CLI on a slice, and the hero line then says "first N of M chars". The placeholder limit here is 5,000. Proposal for the under-limit case: the text region scrolls inside the card, the card never grows past the screen (PopupCard caps it), the inspector and footer stay pinned, and the focused mark scrolls into view as you move with Up and Down. The hard ceiling is the CLI limit from HUF-173 (text_too_long), whose number the engine lifecycle ticket sets. Above that the panel shows the error state, not a truncated list.

Tick one for under the limit and one for over the limit.
